The Crystal approach

A beauty house built around how you want to feel.

Crystal Beauty Center brings hair, skin, nails and bridal artistry together under one roof, with two branches in Muscat, at Al Khuwair and Al Mouj.

Every appointment begins with a conversation. We listen to what you love, what you would like to change, and how you want to feel when you leave, then shape the service around it.

  • 01

    Hair & colour

    Cuts, colour, keratin, botox and specialised treatments for polished, healthy-looking hair.

  • 02

    Nails & skin

    Classic and Russian nail care, gel services, facials and pigmentation artistry.

  • 03

    Bridal & makeup

    Bridal hair and makeup, occasion looks, plus brow and lash finishing.
